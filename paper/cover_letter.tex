\noindent\textbf{Subject:} Submission of manuscript ``A Comparative Performance Analysis of Machine Learning Models for E-Sports Match Outcome Prediction'' (ICDPN 2026, double-blind review)

\bigskip

\noindent Dear Program Chairs,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript for consideration at ICDPN 2026.

The paper presents a systematic comparison of three classical machine-learning classifiers---Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost---on the League of Legends Diamond ranked matches 10-minute dataset. The study evaluates these models under a consistent experimental protocol, analysing why early-game features lead to comparable performance across both linear and non-linear methods.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Anonymization for double-blind review.}
To comply with double-blind review, all identifying information has been removed from the manuscript. The paper does not contain author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or any other identifying information that could compromise the blind review process.

The repository containing the code and data will be made publicly available upon acceptance. We are happy to make the repository available to the editors at their discretion for verification, and to all readers upon acceptance.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Originality and exclusivity.} This manuscript is original work, has not been published previously, and is not under concurrent review at any other venue. All experimental results are reproducible from the referenced dataset and the provided code.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Relevance to ICDPN.} This work is directly relevant to the conference themes of data processing and machine learning applications. It provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item A rigorous empirical comparison of standard ML classifiers on a real-world esports dataset.
\item Insights into the limitations of early-game prediction in competitive gaming.
\item A reproducible experimental methodology that can serve as a baseline for future studies.
\end{itemize}
